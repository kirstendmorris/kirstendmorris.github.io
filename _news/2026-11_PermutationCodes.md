New preprint: *Permutation codes via spectral graph theory*, with Aida Abiad, Delio Jaramillo-Velez and Joachim Rosenthal, is now on the [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14819). We use the Eigenvalue Method to obtain upper spectral bounds on permutation codes in the Hamming, Ulam, Cayley and Kendall metrics, improving the double-ball bound of Buzaglo and Etzion in the Kendall metric and the sphere-packing bound in the Cayley metric.
